Monthly report.

A short report every month on what went out and how it did across every channel, and we go through it together on the next ideation call.

What should I expect by month 3 and month 6?

By month 3, your brand and site are live and content has been going out for a few weeks, with your newsletter in people’s inboxes. By month 6, you have months of steady publishing and a clear read on which topics bring real conversations. We agree on what success looks like at kickoff and measure against that.

How outreach runs.

We find the people your goals depend on, open a real conversation in your voice, and follow up until it becomes a meeting. Outreach launches once your website and LinkedIn are live, so everyone we reach finds a brand that matches the message.

Any reply stops both channels, and a positive reply gets your booking link. No email address means LinkedIn only; not on LinkedIn means email only, on the same days. Opt-outs end all contact. Days, volume and wording are set in your playbook.
