Agreement

These Master Services Agreement terms (“Agreement”) govern all services provided by BW Missions Inc. dba Arcbound (“Arcbound”) to any client (“Client”) who executes an Order Form referencing this Agreement. By signing an Order Form that incorporates this Agreement by reference, Client agrees to be bound by these terms.

Arcbound provides strategic website, content, graphic design, brand development, brand activation, and audio visual production services. The specific services applicable to each Client are determined exclusively by the applicable Order Form.

Section 01

Definitions

The following defined terms apply throughout this Agreement, all Order Forms, and all Exhibits. Where a term is used in an Exhibit, it carries the meaning defined here unless the Exhibit explicitly provides otherwise.

(a) “Agreement” means these Master Services Agreement terms, together with all executed Order Forms, Exhibits, and Change Orders.

(b) “Arcbase Services” means ongoing brand activation services provided as a managed service following a completed Arc Launch, whereby Arcbound assumes operational responsibility for Client’s monthly newsletter and up to three (3) LinkedIn posts per week, as subscribed in the relevant Order Form. Arcbound handles the complete workflow from monthly ideation sessions through content creation, publication, and performance monitoring, with Client’s role limited to strategic input during ideation calls and approval opportunities as specified in Section 2.3(a)(ii).

(c) “Arcbound Materials” means all documents, data, know-how, methodologies, tools, processes, systems, and other materials created, developed, or acquired by Arcbound independently of this Agreement, including any work product created in connection with the Services that is not accepted or used by Client and/or not included as part of the final Deliverables.

(d) “Business Day” means Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM in Client’s primary business location time zone, excluding U.S. federal holidays.

(e) “Change Order” means a written modification to an Order Form agreed upon by authorized representatives of both parties, as further described in Section 2.5.

(f) “Client Materials” means any documents, data, information, specifications, content, photographs, and other materials that Client provides or makes available to Arcbound for use in connection with this Agreement.

(g) “Content Batch” means a collection of assets provided to Client in advance of publication, based on the entitlements of the applicable Order Form. A Content Batch includes a minimum of two weeks’ worth of monthly brand activation assets.

(h) “Deliverables” means all completed work product, designs, content, videos, and other materials prepared by or on behalf of Arcbound in the course of performing the Services and accepted and paid for by Client, including any items identified as such in each Order Form.

(i) “Feedback Window” means seven (7) Business Days from the date Client receives a Content Batch or draft deliverable. If Client does not provide feedback within the Feedback Window, the content will be deemed approved and Arcbound may proceed to publication as scheduled, except for Red Box Content (Section 1(j)) and documented emergencies (Section 2.4(d)).

(j) “Red Box Content” means content that Arcbound identifies as potentially sensitive or that Client designates as requiring explicit approval. Red Box Content is never subject to deemed approval. It includes:

Personal Topics including health issues, family matters, personal struggles, relationships;

Financial Information including revenue figures, business performance, proprietary data;

Legal Matters including ongoing disputes, litigation, complaints, regulatory issues;

Controversial Positions including political stances, divisive social issues, competitor claims;

Major Announcements including new products, partnerships, organizational changes;

Regulated Content including health claims, financial advice, professional services claims;

Client-Designated Topics which are any additional topics Client identifies during onboarding or thereafter as excluded from the deemed approval workflow.

Arcbound will mark Red Box Content “RED BOX – EXPLICIT APPROVAL REQUIRED” and will not publish without Client’s written approval. Client has fifteen (15) Business Days to review. After fifteen Business Days without response, Arcbound will send one reminder, then remove the content from the schedule.

(k) “Intellectual Property Rights” means any rights associated with intellectual property, including (i) rights in patents and inventions, (ii) trademarks, service marks, trade names, logos and corporate names (whether registered or unregistered), including all associated goodwill, (iii) copyrights (registered or unregistered) and works of authorship, (iv) computer software, data, databases and documentation thereof, (v) trade secrets, and (vi) all rights, remedies against infringements, and rights to protect such interests.

(l) “Order Form” means a signed order document executed by both parties that identifies the Services to be provided, applicable fees, the term of engagement, and incorporates this Agreement by reference. Each Order Form is a separate binding agreement subject to these terms.

(m) “Publication” means the act of making content publicly visible on Client’s social media accounts, website, or other public-facing platforms. Content is considered published when it becomes accessible to the public, regardless of whether it has been promoted or widely distributed.

(n) “Representatives” means a party’s employees, officers, directors, agents, contractors, and consultants.

(o) “Services” means the brand-building and brand-activation services Arcbound will provide to Client as described in one or more Order Forms. Any services not expressly included in an applicable Order Form are outside the scope of the Services.

(p) “Standard Revision” means one round of revisions per Content Batch or deliverable, covering minor adjustments to narrative content, visual elements, or formatting that do not substantially alter the scope or direction of the work. Any revision request beyond this one included round constitutes an Additional Round of Revision and is subject to additional fees per Exhibit C.

(q) “Third-Party Materials” means materials and information, in any form or medium, including any software (including open-source software), documents, data, content, specifications, products, equipment, or components incorporated into the Deliverables or used by Arcbound in connection with providing the Services that are not proprietary to Arcbound.

Section 02

Services and Obligations of the Parties

2.1 The Services

Arcbound will provide the Services as outlined in one or more Order Forms. Each Order Form operates independently. Any services not included in an applicable Order Form are outside the scope of the Services. For Clients subscribed to Arcbase Services as defined in Exhibit F, the parties acknowledge that Arcbase is designed as a streamlined, efficient managed service with strictly defined boundaries. Services explicitly excluded in Exhibit F cannot be added to Arcbase Services through informal requests, verbal agreements, or course of dealing. Any expansion of Arcbase Services requires a formal Change Order or new Order Form executed by both parties.

2.2 Arcbound Obligations

Arcbound will provide the Services in a professional, diligent, and commercially reasonable manner. Arcbound reserves the right to hire and replace personnel to assist in performing the Services, and will remain responsible for their performance, payment, and compliance with this Agreement. Client acknowledges that Arcbound is free to provide similar services to other parties, including those competitive with Client, so long as there is no deemed potential harm to Client as a result.

2.3 Client Engagement Requirements and Remedies

(a) Essential Client Obligations

Client acknowledges that the following obligations are essential to successful service delivery:

(i) Participation and Cooperation. Attending scheduled discovery, ideation, scoping, planning, and delivery sessions as defined in the applicable Order Form or Exhibit; cooperating in all matters relating to the Services; and providing sufficient information, visual media, and documentation as reasonably necessary.

(ii) Timely Feedback. Providing feedback within the Feedback Window (Section 1(i)). Content not reviewed within the Feedback Window will be deemed approved and published as scheduled, except for Red Box Content (Section 1(j)) and documented emergencies (Section 2.4(d)).

(iii) Revision Requests. Requesting revisions within the initial Feedback Window. Client is entitled to one Standard Revision (Section 1(p)) per Content Batch or arc launch asset. Additional Rounds of Revision are subject to additional fees per Exhibit C.

(iv) Provision of Materials. Providing complete and accurate Client Materials that Arcbound may request in a timely manner, including access credentials, brand assets, and approvals.

(v) Payment Obligations. Making timely payment of all fees and expenses as specified in Section 4.

(vi) Point of Contact. Designating and maintaining a primary point of contact with decision-making authority who will be reasonably available.

(b) Graduated Remedy Framework

Arcbound will address Client’s failure to fulfill Essential Client Obligations through the following graduated process:

InstanceArcbound ResponseCure Period
FirstWritten notice via email10 Business Days; no penalty if cured
Second (within 6 months)Written notice + discretion to cancel Services, proceed on Arcbound’s judgment, or publish using Arcbound’s judgment (except Red Box Content)10 Business Days; fees continue during pause
Third (within 6 months)Written notice + termination effective 30 days from notice, or cancellation until renewed engagementSee Section 2.3(c) for material breach consequences

(i) First Instance. Upon the first failure to fulfill any Essential Client Obligation, Arcbound will provide written notice via email. Client will have ten (10) Business Days to cure. No penalty applies if cured within this period.

(ii) Second Instance. Upon a second failure within any six (6) month period, Arcbound will provide written notice and may, at its discretion: (A) cancel Services until the issue is resolved; (B) continue Services using Arcbound’s professional judgment where Client input would normally be required, with prior written notice; or (C) for cumulative content feedback failures, proceed with Publication using Arcbound’s professional judgment (except for Red Box Content). Client will have ten (10) Business Days to cure. Services cancelled under this provision will not extend the Initial Term, and Client remains obligated to pay all fees during any pause period.

(iii) Third Instance. Upon a third failure within any six (6) month period, Arcbound may, upon written notice: (A) terminate this Agreement and any Order Form effective thirty (30) days from the date of notice; or (B) cancel all Services until Client demonstrates renewed engagement, as determined reasonably and in good faith by Arcbound.

(c) Material Breach

A pattern of three (3) or more failures to fulfill Essential Client Obligations within any six (6) month period constitutes a material breach of this Agreement. Upon material breach, if Arcbound elects to terminate: (i) Client will pay all fees and expenses incurred through the termination date; and (ii) Client will pay the equivalent of sixty (60) days of fees from the termination date.

(d) Effect of Delays

Arcbound is not liable for any costs, delays, or losses caused by Client’s acts or omissions. Arcbound may adjust deadlines or terminate Client’s entitlement to content for periods when feedback or approval is not provided within the Feedback Window. Client remains responsible for all fees during such periods.

(e) Immediate Termination Rights

Notwithstanding the graduated framework above, Arcbound may terminate immediately upon written notice if:

Client fails to pay any invoice within forty-five (45) days of the due date;

Client provides false or materially misleading information;

Client violates Sections 9.12 or 9.13 (Ethics and Conduct); or

Client’s actions create legal or reputational risk to Arcbound.

(f) Documentation

All notices under this Section must be in writing via email to the contacts specified in Section 9.5. Arcbound will maintain records of all instances of non-compliance and remediation efforts.

2.4 Mutual Obligations; Good Faith Collaboration

(a) Mutual Commitment. Both parties commit to good faith collaboration, recognizing that brand building requires ongoing partnership and communication.

(b) Proactive Communication. If either party anticipates difficulty meeting an obligation (travel, illness, resource constraints, etc.), that party will notify the other as soon as reasonably possible to discuss accommodations.

(c) Reasonable Accommodations. Arcbound will work reasonably with Client to accommodate documented emergencies, business travel, and other legitimate circumstances that may affect Client’s ability to meet standard timelines, provided Client communicates proactively.

(d) Documented Emergencies. For purposes of Section 2.3, “documented emergencies” include: serious illness or injury (Client or immediate family); natural disasters affecting Client’s location; unexpected business crises requiring immediate attention; and other circumstances agreed to in writing by Arcbound. Client requesting accommodation should provide reasonable notice.

(e) No Abuse. This Section is not intended to excuse chronic non-engagement or serve as a blanket waiver of Client’s obligations. Accommodations are made in good faith and at Arcbound’s discretion.

2.5 Change Orders

If at any time Client requests a modification to the Services, Client must submit a written Change Order request. Within seven (7) days of receiving the request, Arcbound will provide a written response outlining: (i) any change in fees; (ii) the impact on anticipated milestones or completion dates; and (iii) the availability of Arcbound’s resources to carry out the change. Arcbound may in its sole discretion refuse any proposed Change Order. Any agreed Change Order must be signed by both parties and is deemed incorporated into the applicable Order Form and this Agreement.

Section 03

Term and Termination

3.1 Agreement Term

This Agreement commences on the date Client first executes an Order Form incorporating it by reference, and remains in effect while any Order Form executed under it is active. The Agreement terminates automatically thirty (30) days after all Order Forms have expired or been terminated. Sections 3 through 8 survive termination.

3.2 Order Form Term

(a) Initial Term. Each Order Form will specify its own start date and duration (“Initial Term”). Multiple Order Forms may be active simultaneously. Termination or expiration of one Order Form does not affect other active Order Forms or this Agreement.

(b) Renewal. Upon expiration of the Initial Term, and upon expiration of any Renewal Term thereafter, each Order Form for recurring work shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year terms (“Renewal Term”), unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to the end of the then-current term. Fees for each Renewal Term shall increase by seven percent (7%) over the prior term’s fees.

3.3 Termination

(a) Termination for Cause. Either party (the “Non-Breaching Party”) may terminate this Agreement or any Order Form if the other party (the “Breaching Party”) materially breaches its obligations and, after receiving written notice identifying such breach in reasonable detail, fails to cure within sixty (60) days from the date of notice; provided that in the case of a breach that cannot reasonably be cured within sixty (60) days, the Non-Breaching Party may terminate only if the Breaching Party has failed to commence substantial remedial actions within such sixty (60) day period and diligently pursue the same to completion.

Section 04

Fees and Expenses

4.1 Service Fees

Client shall pay Arcbound the fees set forth in each Order Form. Where Services are provided for a fixed price, fees are payable as a lump sum or installment payments as stated in the applicable Order Form. Client agrees to reimburse Arcbound for all reasonable out-of-pocket expenses approved in advance.

4.2 Invoices

Unless otherwise specified in an applicable Order Form, Arcbound will invoice Client on a monthly basis, or upon completion of a given project. Client will pay all fees within fifteen (15) days after receipt of each invoice. Unless otherwise agreed in writing, Client will make payments by wire transfer, credit card, or other mutually acceptable payment method.

4.3 Late Fees

If Client fails to pay all or any portion of fees within forty-five (45) days from the invoice date, the unpaid amount shall bear interest at 1.5% per month (18% per annum), or the maximum rate permitted under applicable law, whichever is less. Interest accrues from the original invoice due date (fifteen (15) days after invoice date) until payment is received in full. Arcbound reserves the right to suspend all Services until any overdue amounts and applicable late fees are paid in full. Suspension does not relieve Client of payment obligations for Services already performed or waive Arcbound’s right to terminate under Section 2.3(e)(i).

Section 05

Confidentiality and Non-Solicitation

5.1 Confidential Information

Both parties agree to treat as confidential the terms and conditions of this Agreement, and all information provided by each party to the other regarding its past, present, and future business affairs, operations, plans, intellectual property, trade secrets, know-how, practices, and commercial strategies. Confidential information shall be used only for the purpose of rendering or obtaining the Services, or as otherwise expressly permitted under this Agreement. Each party shall hold the other party’s confidential information in strict confidence and shall not disclose it to any third party without the other party’s prior written consent. These obligations do not apply to information that is or becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Agreement, and continue during the term and for one (1) year after the termination date.

5.2 Mutual Non-Solicitation

During the term of this Agreement and for one (1) year after the termination date, neither party shall, directly or indirectly: (a) employ or hire; (b) knowingly permit any entity under its direct or indirect control to employ or hire; (c) recruit, solicit, attempt to hire, interfere with, or endeavor to entice away; or (d) assist any entity to recruit or solicit, any person who is or was employed by the other party during the term of this Agreement. This restriction excludes employees or contractors of either party who apply independently and without solicitation.

To the extent any Deliverables do not constitute a “work made for hire,” Arcbound hereby assigns all right, title, and interest in and to the Deliverables, including all associated Intellectual Property Rights, to Client.

6.2 Third-Party Materials

The Deliverables may include or operate in conjunction with Third-Party Materials. Arcbound will notify Client of all Third-Party Materials included in or required for use with any Deliverable on or prior to delivery. All Third-Party Materials are provided pursuant to the terms of an applicable third-party license agreement, and Arcbound will provide Client with a copy of any such agreement if requested. Client shall comply with all applicable third-party licenses, and any breach by Client of any such license will be deemed a breach of this Agreement. Ownership of all Third-Party Materials and corresponding Intellectual Property Rights remains with the respective owners, subject to any express licenses or sublicenses granted to Client.

6.3 Arcbound Materials

Arcbound and its licensors are, and will remain, the sole and exclusive owners of all right, title, and interest in and to the Arcbound Materials, including all associated Intellectual Property Rights, and the processes by which it provides the Services. Arcbound hereby grants Client a limited, royalty-free, non-transferable, non-sublicensable, worldwide license to use, display, and reproduce Arcbound Materials to the extent incorporated in or necessary for the use of the Deliverables. Arcbound expressly reserves all other rights in and to the Arcbound Materials.

6.4 Client Materials

Client and its licensors are, and will remain, the sole and exclusive owners of all right, title, and interest in and to the Client Materials, including all associated Intellectual Property Rights. Client hereby grants Arcbound a royalty-free, non-exclusive license to use, reproduce, perform, display, distribute, modify, and create derivative works of the Client Materials in order to develop the Deliverables and perform the Services.

Section 07

Indemnification

7.1 Mutual Indemnification

Each party (as “Indemnifying Party”) agrees to indemnify and hold the other party harmless from any and all costs, damages, liabilities, losses, and expenses related to any claim or action by a third party arising out of or resulting from: (a) breach by the Indemnifying Party or any of its Representatives of any term or condition of this Agreement; or (b) gross negligence or more culpable act or omission of the Indemnifying Party.

7.2 Limitation of Liability

TO THE MAXIMUM EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, ARCBOUND’S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT, WHETHER IN CONTRACT, TORT, OR OTHERWISE, SHALL NOT EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES PAID BY CLIENT TO ARCBOUND UNDER THE APPLICABLE ORDER FORM DURING THE THREE (3) MONTHS PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM. IN NO EVENT SHALL ARCBOUND BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOST PROFITS, LOSS OF REVENUE, LOSS OF DATA, OR LOSS OF BUSINESS OPPORTUNITIES, EVEN IF ARCBOUND HAS BEEN ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES.

7.3 Social Media Disclaimers

Client acknowledges that: (a) social media platforms may change algorithms, policies, or features without notice; (b) Arcbound cannot guarantee specific reach, engagement, or growth metrics; (c) Client is responsible for final approval of all content before Publication unless Client has failed to provide feedback within the Feedback Window; and (d) Arcbound is not responsible for negative comments, platform outages, account suspensions due to platform policy changes, or other third-party actions beyond Arcbound’s reasonable control.

Section 08

Injunctive Relief

Each party acknowledges that a breach of Section 5 (Confidentiality and Non-Solicitation) or Section 6 (Ownership of Deliverables; Intellectual Property Rights) may cause the non-breaching party irreparable damages for which an award of damages would not be adequate compensation. Each party agrees that in the event of such breach or threatened breach, the non-breaching party will be entitled to seek equitable relief, including injunctive relief, specific performance, and any other relief that may be available. Such remedies shall not be exclusive but shall be in addition to all other remedies available at law or in equity.

Section 09

Miscellaneous

9.1 Relationship of the Parties; No Exclusivity

The relationship between the parties is that of independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement will be construed as creating any agency, partnership, joint venture, employment, or fiduciary relationship, and neither party will have authority to contract for or bind the other party in any manner. No exclusive relationship is formed by this Agreement. Either party may engage or be engaged by a third party in a similar relationship to that contemplated under this Agreement.

9.2 Assignment

Neither party may assign or transfer its rights or delegate its obligations under this Agreement without the other party’s prior written consent. Any purported assignment or delegation in violation of this Section is null and void. This Agreement will be binding upon the permitted successors and assigns of the parties.

9.3 Force Majeure

Neither party shall be liable for any loss or delay resulting from any force majeure event, including acts of God, fire, natural disaster, labor stoppage, war or military hostilities, or inability of carriers to make scheduled deliveries, or from social media platform outages, policy changes, account suspensions, or API limitations. Any milestone or delivery date shall be extended to the extent of any delay resulting from a force majeure event.

9.4 Entire Agreement; Order of Precedence; Amendment

This Agreement, together with all executed Order Forms, Exhibits, and Change Orders, constitutes the sole and entire agreement between the parties regarding the Services. In the event of any conflict or inconsistency: (a) between this Agreement and an Order Form, the Agreement controls unless the Order Form explicitly states it supersedes a specific Agreement provision; (b) between Order Forms, each is independent and provisions in one do not affect others; (c) between Exhibits, the most recently dated Exhibit controls. This Agreement and any Order Form may be amended only by a writing signed by authorized representatives of both parties.

9.5 Notices

Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement, notices given pursuant to this Agreement will be sufficient if given in writing and delivered by email with confirmation of receipt to the contacts specified in the applicable Order Form.

9.6 Updates to These Terms

Arcbound reserves the right to update or modify this Agreement at any time. Arcbound will provide at least thirty (30) days’ advance written notice of any material changes to Clients with active Order Forms, delivered by email to the contact specified in the applicable Order Form. The updated terms will take effect at the end of the notice period. A Client’s continued engagement with Arcbound following the notice period constitutes acceptance of the updated terms. The version of this Agreement in effect at the time an Order Form is executed governs that Order Form unless both parties agree in writing to apply updated terms. All versions of this Agreement are identified by the “Last Updated” date in the header.

9.7 Waiver

No failure or delay by either party in exercising any right or remedy under this Agreement will operate or be deemed as a waiver of any such right or remedy. The waiver of a breach of this Agreement will in no event constitute a waiver as to any other breach or prevent the exercise of any right under this Agreement.

9.8 No Third-Party Beneficiaries

This Agreement is for the sole benefit of Client and Arcbound, and their respective Representatives and permitted successors and assigns. Nothing in this Agreement is intended to confer upon any other person or entity any legal or equitable right, benefit, or remedy of any nature whatsoever.

9.9 Severability

If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid, unenforceable, or illegal, that provision may be severed, and such invalidity, unenforceability, or illegality shall not affect any other provisions of this Agreement.

9.10 Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the state of California. All actions or proceedings relating to this Agreement shall be maintained in a court located in the City and County of San Francisco, California, and the parties consent to the jurisdiction of said court and waive any objection to said venue. The parties shall attempt to resolve any dispute or claim in good faith before pursuing formal proceedings. This Agreement is intended for business-to-business engagements and is not directed at consumers.

9.11 Survival

Provisions of this Agreement which by their nature should apply beyond their terms will remain in force after any termination or expiration, including, but not limited to, Sections 3 through 8. Arcbound will be entitled to receipt of pro rata payment for Services rendered prior to termination.

9.12 Counterparts

Order Forms executed under this Agreement may be executed in any number of counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original. Execution by electronic or digital signature will have the same force and effect as execution of an original.

9.13 Code of Ethics, Conduct & Values

Arcbound does not tolerate racist, supremacist, sexist, misogynist, homophobic, transphobic, discriminatory, inappropriately sexual, or other unethical conduct or language by clients either toward our team members or expressed publicly. Violations of this policy will result in the ceasing of work and termination of all active Order Forms.

9.14 Ethical Content Creation Policy

Arcbound will not create or post content on behalf of clients that is racist, supremacist, sexist, misogynist, homophobic, transphobic, discriminatory, inappropriately sexual, or otherwise unethical, regardless of an expressed request from the client. Should a client persist in these requests, Arcbound will cease work and terminate all active Order Forms.
